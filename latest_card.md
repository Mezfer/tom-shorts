# How Penguins Process Seawater

https://youtube.com/shorts/TIToAYktelY  |  slot: comparison_puzzle  |  series: Sea Abilities That Sound Fake

**Idea:** Penguins have specialized glands above their eyes that filter salt out of seawater, allowing them to drink it safely.
**Hook (curiosity-raising science question):** Why don't penguins get thirsty in the ocean?
**Why chosen:** It solves a common biological riddle about marine life.

## Script
Why don't penguins get thirsty in the ocean? They spend their lives surrounded by salty water that would be dangerous for humans to drink. The secret is hidden right above their eyes. Penguins have specialized glands that extract excess salt from their bloodstream. These organs act like a built-in desalination system. They remove salt from the blood after the penguin ingests seawater. This helps many species manage salt intake while at sea. Do you know how they excrete this concentrated brine?
