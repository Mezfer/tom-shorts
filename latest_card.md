**Idea:** Coral polyps live in a symbiotic relationship with zooxanthellae algae that provide them with food through photosynthesis.
**Hook (curiosity-raising science question):** Is a coral reef an animal or a plant?
**Why chosen:** Addresses a very common scientific misconception.

## Script
Are coral reefs animals or plants? It seems like a trick, but the answer is surprising. Coral polyps are animals that possess a unique biological strategy. Many species maintain a symbiotic relationship with algae called zooxanthellae. The coral hosts these algae, which provide nutrients through photosynthesis. Would you host your food source inside your own body to survive?
