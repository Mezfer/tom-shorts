# How Stingrays Detect Hidden Prey

**Idea:** Stingrays use electrical sensors called ampullae of Lorenzini to detect the heartbeat of hidden prey.
**Hook (unexpected ability):** Stingrays can 'hear' your heartbeat.
**Why chosen:** The concept of 'hearing a heartbeat' is immediately gripping for a general audience.

## Script
Stingrays can detect your heartbeat. It seems like a superpower, but they are actually hunting for prey buried under the sand. These animals use specialized sensory organs called ampullae of Lorenzini. These sensors detect the weak bioelectric fields generated by living hearts. This allows them to track meals that are invisible to the naked eye. Could you hide from them?
